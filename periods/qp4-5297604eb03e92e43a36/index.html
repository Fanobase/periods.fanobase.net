CKP(129) — Coates–Kasprzyk–Prince (2015)

The paper’s representative complete intersection in a smooth toric Fano ambient variety. The matrix records the toric weights on the left and the degrees of the defining line bundles on the right.

Method. Toric complete-intersection mirror theorem; Mirror Laurent polynomial; constant-term extraction.

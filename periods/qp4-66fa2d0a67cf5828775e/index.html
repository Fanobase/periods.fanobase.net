Published descriptions

CKP(43) — Coates–Kasprzyk–Prince (2015)

The paper’s representative complete intersection in a smooth toric Fano ambient variety. The matrix records the toric weights on the left and the degrees of the defining line bundles on the right.

Method. Toric complete-intersection mirror theorem; Mirror Laurent polynomial; constant-term extraction.

Toric weights | defining line bundles:

111102
000011

Operator. Lairez’s Griffiths–Dwork algorithm, followed by reconstruction and recurrence checks.

Appendix, index-2 construction table, period 43. Agreement of 32 coefficients, compared with Zenodo reference.

The quiver flag zero locus used as the representative in the paper. Its ambient quiver flag variety is specified by the adjacency matrix and dimension vector; the generalized partitions specify the defining homogeneous bundle.

Method. Abelian/non-Abelian correspondence for quiver flag zero loci; Hypergeometric series and quantum Lefschetz.

The paper’s representative complete intersection in a smooth toric Fano ambient variety. The matrix records the toric weights on the left and the degrees of the defining line bundles on the right.

About the collection

The catalogue brings together factorial-regularized quantum periods of smooth Fano fourfolds. The public edition contains CKP, Kalashnikov and type-a.fanobase.net. The private working edition adds the other captured research datasets. Each edition generates its own pages, search index and downloads from the datasets it contains.

Finding and discussing a period

Search by a paper label, type-A family label, construction label, period ID or AESZ/CYDB reference. You can also paste up to 20 initial coefficients, starting at degree zero. Choose a dataset to see its periods; open More filters to compare included and excluded datasets. Searches have shareable URLs, and CSV exports follow the current filters.

Each row groups labels whose recorded coefficients agree. Open a period to see every dataset label and the precision recorded for it. Copy label + period gives plain text in the form CKP(23): [1, 0, …], using the full available sequence. Copy link gives a stable reference to the grouped period page. The datasets page provides label + period downloads for each dataset.

Coefficients and grouping

Arrays start at degree zero. Degree 100 means 101 coefficients, including the constant coefficient one; the linear coefficient is zero. Unknown coefficients of a shorter prefix are never filled with zeros.

Grouping compares all available exact coefficients, without rescaling. A shorter observation attaches only when at least 16 coefficients agree with a unique longer vector, or when it has an explicitly certified direct continuation of the same construction. Ambiguous short prefixes stay separate and link to compatible longer records. Finite agreement does not establish equality of infinite series or deformation equivalence of geometric constructions.

Dataset membership follows the recorded presentation. In particular, quiver moduli spaces themselves and complete intersections inside quiver moduli have separate dataset identities, even when coefficients agree. Tentative formal series in the working edition are clearly labelled and excluded from directly computed quantum-period downloads.

Operators and comparisons

Type-A scalar operators are derived by exact regularization and elimination from the computed quantum connection. Original published operator proof flags remain source metadata. Exact operator comparisons are recorded separately; agreement of coefficient prefixes alone never upgrades proof status.

The Calabi–Yau comparisons record the coordinate changes, comparison lengths, operator evidence and reported source invariants from a pinned Mainz CYDB snapshot. A period or operator match does not identify a CY3 deformation family.

Captured data

The snapshot date appears in the footer. The site uses saved data and does not poll running computations. Search and filtering run in the browser; period pages and downloads also work without JavaScript.

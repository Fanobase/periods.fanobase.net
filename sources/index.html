Published datasets

CKP
Coates–Kasprzyk–Prince (2015), Four-dimensional Fano toric complete intersections (arXiv:1409.5030).

Toric complete intersections, labelled CKP(1)–CKP(738). The period pages give toric weights, defining line bundles and the mirror method.

Kalashnikov
Kalashnikov (2019), Four-dimensional Fano quiver flag zero loci (arXiv:1808.00311).

Quiver flag zero loci, labelled CKK(1)–CKK(749). The period pages give adjacency matrices, dimension vectors, generalized partitions and the Abelian/non-Abelian method.

type-a.fanobase.net

Zero loci on products of type-A flag varieties and Grassmannians, with the family labels used on the type-A database. Exact localization and quantum-connection computations supply the coefficients. Period pages link to the corresponding families.

Archive and conventions

The published data were captured from the Coates–Kasprzyk archive, Zenodo 5708307 (CC0). The original papers remain the dataset references. Archive IDs, supplied term counts and operator proof flags are retained on the period pages.

Arrays start at degree zero and use factorial-regularized anticanonical coefficients. Degree 100 means 101 coefficients. All available coefficients must agree; shorter prefixes attach only to a unique compatible longer record, or to an explicitly certified continuation of the same construction. Finite agreement does not prove equality of infinite periods or geometric equivalence.

Calabi–Yau threefold comparisons use a pinned Mainz CYDB snapshot and retain the normalization, precision and operator evidence.
